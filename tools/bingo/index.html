One per line: question | answer. You can also paste two columns from a spreadsheet, or type only the answers (12, 7, -3, 0.5, 3/4) if your slides are made somewhere else. Answers can be whole numbers, negatives, decimals, fractions (3/4, 1 1/2) or short text (x = 5). In questions: x^2 powers, x_1 subscripts, sqrt(x), {3}/{4} a stacked fraction, * for ×.
