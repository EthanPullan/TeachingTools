Tips and keyboard shortcuts

  • Choose the number of sides: 3 is a triangle, 4 a quadrilateral (square, rectangle, parallelogram, rhombus, trapezoid, kite), and 5 or more a regular polygon.
  • Pick Measurements to make an exact shape, or Dragging corners to shape one by hand. Dragging a corner from Measurements keeps the shape as it is; Undo brings back the measurements.
  • In Dragging corners you can also type a side length or an angle, and choose which end or side moves. Lock sides to keep their lengths while you drag.
  • On the Labels tab, set a side or angle to Unknown to make a worksheet: the question shows “?”, the key shows the answer.
  • Tab to a corner ring and use the arrow keys to move it (hold Shift for bigger steps).
  • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · Ctrl+S save.
  • Equal sides, equal angles and parallel sides are found automatically and marked with ticks, arcs and arrowheads.
