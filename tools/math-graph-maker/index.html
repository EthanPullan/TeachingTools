Math Graph Maker graphs for math tests

What are you making?

Bar, line, circle, picto-, histogram, dot, stem-and-leaf, box and scatter graphs from a list of data. Enter it on the Data tab.

Blank grid, dot and isometric paper for a whole page, or several small grids per page. Set it up on the Paper tab.

Set up the line on the Number line tab. Your plane objects are kept, and come back if you switch again.

A lone x or y is italic automatically. Wrap other variables in stars, like *t*; write powers as x^2. A hyphen before a number becomes a true minus sign.

Grid layout

Grid squares are always square: a square is one step between numbered gridlines. Set a different range on the Axes tab.

Printed size

Sizes are real print sizes. The width is fixed; leave the height blank and it follows the grid so squares stay square.

Physical scale

The page grows to fit the grid, so the size above is ignored. Paste the PNG into Word at 100% (it is stamped 300 DPI) and a ruler will measure true.

Quick setup

Sets everything below. Undo brings back what you had.

Paper

10 mm is a 1 cm square; 6.35 mm is ¼ inch. Only whole squares are drawn, centred on the page.

Page

The title (Graph tab) is written at the top of the page.

Grids per page

1 across and 1 down is a full page. Use 2 × 2 or 2 × 3 with axes for practice graphs.

Printing

Use Export PNG (stamped 300 DPI, prints at exactly the paper size) and print it at 100%, or save the SVG. The page is not an answer key, so there is only one version.

You can also click on the graph: turn on “Click to add points” above the preview. Drag a point or a corner to move it. In the table, paste a block copied from Excel or Google Sheets into any cell (label, x, y or just x, y). Enter moves down. Red cells aren’t numbers and are left off the graph.

Paste points from a spreadsheet

Copy cells in Excel or Google Sheets and paste them here. Two columns are x and y; with a third column of names (A, B, C…) put the names first. A header row is skipped.
