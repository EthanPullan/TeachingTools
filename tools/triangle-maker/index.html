Tips and keyboard shortcuts

  • Pick Measurements to make an exact triangle from what you know, or Dragging corners to shape one by hand.
  • Dragging a corner while in Measurements switches to Dragging corners, keeping the triangle as it is. Undo brings back the measurements.
  • On the Labels tab, set a side or angle to Unknown to make a worksheet: the question shows “?”, the key shows the answer.
  • Tab to a corner ring and use the arrow keys to move it (hold Shift for bigger steps).
  • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · Ctrl+S save.
  • Equal sides and equal angles are found automatically, to within 0.1%, and marked with ticks and arcs.
